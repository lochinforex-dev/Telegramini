# Privacy Policy — FastMessenger

_Last updated: 25 October 2026_

FastMessenger ("the app") is an **unofficial** messaging client. It is built on the
open-source code of Telegram for Android and uses the Telegram API. The app is not
made, endorsed or supported by Telegram FZ-LLC.

## 2. How your data is handled

The app connects directly to Telegram's servers. Your phone number, messages,
media, contacts, groups, channels and account settings are sent to and stored by
Telegram, and are handled according to Telegram's own Privacy Policy:
https://telegram.org/privacy

The developer of FastMessenger does **not** operate its own servers for messages,
and does **not** receive, read, store or sell your messages, contacts or other
personal content. The developer does not add its own analytics, advertising or
tracking code to the app.

## 3. Data stored on your device

Like the original client, the app stores data locally on your device so that it can
work: a cache of chats and media, account sessions, and app settings. You can clear
this data at any time from the app's settings or by removing the app.

## 4. Permissions

The app may ask for permissions such as contacts, storage, camera, microphone,
location and notifications. They are used only for the features you choose to use
(for example, sending a photo or a voice message) and are handled as described in
section 2.

## 5. Notifications

Like the original client, the app can use Google Firebase Cloud Messaging to
deliver push notifications. This service is provided by Google and is subject to
Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 6. Children

The app is not intended for children under the minimum age required by Telegram's
Terms of Service.
